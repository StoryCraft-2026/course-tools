How it works

Four steps from wishlist to Pitch Report.

Pick a Pitch Day weekend, filter by genre, read wishlists and tap the open times you want into your cart. Want longer with one agent? Book back-to-back times.

Check out

Your times are held for 20 minutes while you pay. Checkout sets up your StoryCraft account if you don't have one yet.

Get ready

Sign in to My Pitch Day to add your book details for your agents and watch the short welcome video on how the day runs. You can change times until your schedule locks 7 days before.

Pitch and get your report

A producer checks your camera and mic in the green room, then moves you into the agent's room. Pitch in 5 minutes or less, then talk. Your Pitch Report arrives within 48 hours.

The agents

Pick your weekend, your agents and your times.

Pitches run 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM Pacific (10:00 AM to 9:00 PM Eastern) both days. Each time is 15 minutes: 5 in the green room with a producer, then 10 with the agent. Want to pitch a second book or ask more questions? Book back-to-back times with the same agent.

Your pages stay human. Every agent we work with has committed not to put writers' query letters, pages or pitches into AI tools. Writers make the same promise in return: everything you share with an agent, from your pitch to your pages, is your own writing, never AI-generated.

New to pitching? You may be a true beginner. That means you've never pitched an agent, or your manuscript isn't finished yet, and you'd like practice or a conversation about your book instead of a formal pitch. Some agents welcome true beginners. Look for the Open to true beginners tag, or turn on the filter below. When you book one of them, tick "I'm a true beginner" in your cart so the agent knows.

Scholarship pitches

Free pitch times for underrepresented writers.

Every agent at Pitch Day takes up to 2 scholarship pitches, free for writers from underrepresented communities, for example BIPOC, LGBTQ+ or disabled writers. StoryCraft pays the agent, so you can apply to pitch any agent on the board. Apply for up to 2 free times. Telling us how you identify is optional and never shared with agents, and scholarship writers look the same as everyone else in the agent's packet.

Scholarships are paid for by StoryCraft and by writers who add $5 to pay it forward at checkout. Anything above the cost of each Pitch Day's scholarships goes into a fund for extra scholarships.

The Pitch Report

Leave every pitch with something you can act on.

After each pitch, the agent fills in a 60-second scorecard. You get one report that brings all of your agents' feedback together.

  • A hook score from every agent, plus notes on your stakes, comps, genre and word count
  • One thing that's working and one thing to change before you query
  • Each agent's next step, and what to send if they request pages
  • A summary across agents, so you can tell a pattern from one person's taste
