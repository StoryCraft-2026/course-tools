What we take off your plate

You show up and listen. We run the rest.

Writers come prepared

Only your genres

We promote you

A producer with you all day

Writers wait in a green room, where a producer checks their camera and mic and brings them into your room. If a writer runs late or a link breaks, your producer sorts it out.

Writers' work stays human.

Every agent at Pitch Day commits to keeping writers' query letters, pages and pitches out of AI tools. We tell writers that promise on the booking page, and it's part of your contract. Writers make a promise too: the pitch and pages they share with you are their own writing, never AI-generated.

How it works

From sign-up to payday.

Before

  1. Fill in the form below.
  2. We email you a short contract.
  3. Once it's signed, you get our ACH payment sign-up with a W-9.
  4. We set up your StoryCraft account. Sign in to your Agent Portal on any device to update your wishlist, bio and hours, or add dates.
  5. Your profile goes live once we launch the event, and writers start booking.
  6. Five days before the event, your schedule and your packet with each writer's one-page appear in your Agent Portal.
  7. If you're doing Live Pre-Read sessions, read those writers' query letters and first 5 pages before the event.

On Pitch Day

  1. Join the StoryCraft Zoom from your schedule 15 minutes before your first time. A producer sets you up in your room.
  2. Your producer brings each writer in from the green room. Writers pitch in 5 minutes or less, and the rest of the 10 minutes is yours for questions and feedback.
  3. Tell each writer yes or no on requesting pages before the call ends.
  4. Fill out the 60-second scorecard in the 5 minutes before your next writer, while it's fresh. The link sits under each writer's one-page.
  5. Message your producer if anything comes up.

After

  1. The evening of Pitch Day, you get your Pitch Day Report: every writer you requested, with their logline, your notes and how they're sending pages.
  2. Writers you request send pages the way you tell us to.
  3. If you signed up for written feedback, we send you query letters or first 5 pages from writers who bought it, so they can revise before they submit. These won't be writers whose pages you requested.
  4. We pay you by ACH within days.

Agreements *

Writers trust us with unpublished work. These promises protect them, and they're written into your contract.

Read the Code of Conduct

Pitch Day only works if writers and agents both feel safe. This policy covers everyone at Pitch Day: writers, agents, producers and StoryCraft staff. It applies in the green room, agent rooms, the First Pages Slam, the Pitch Day forums, and any email or message connected to the event.

Not allowed
  • Harassment of any kind, including comments about someone's race, ethnicity, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, body or appearance
  • Sexual comments, advances or images
  • Threats, insults or shouting at anyone
  • Recording, screenshotting or sharing any session without everyone's consent
  • Contacting a writer or agent in ways they haven't invited, including personal social media accounts and phone numbers
  • Pressuring an agent to change a decision
For agents
  • Don't offer writers your own paid services, such as editing, coaching or consulting, during Pitch Day or in follow-ups.
  • We tell writers that your decisions are final and ask them not to email you to reconsider. If one does, let us know.
Reporting a problem

Tell your producer during the event, or email hello@storycraftacademy.org anytime. We read every report and won't share your name without your permission.

What happens next

We may end a session, remove anyone from Pitch Day without a refund or payment for that session, and bar them from future StoryCraft events.
